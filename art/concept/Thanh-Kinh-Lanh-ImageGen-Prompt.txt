Use case: stylized-concept. Create one wide landscape visual development board for the dark fantasy strategy card game ASHBORNE, proposed direction "chronicle painted on scorched paper". A handcrafted 2D painterly aesthetic: opaque gouache value masses with sparse dry intaglio marks reserved for contour and shadow, tangible stone, faded wax and worn iron, medieval ink cartography. Strong silhouettes, purposeful negative space, restrained saturation, readable at small scale, mournful but quietly resilient. Use warm torchlight only where people still hold ground.
