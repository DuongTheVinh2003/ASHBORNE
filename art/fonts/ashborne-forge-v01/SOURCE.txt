Ashborne Forge v0.1 is an experimental modified derivative, not an original typeface drawn from scratch.
Regular: source weight 500, smooth curves retained for UI.
Semibold: source weight 650, quadratic curves converted to 2-segment facets.
Bold: source weight 800, uppercase and digit quadratic curves converted to 1-segment facets; other curves to 2-segment facets for display.
Accented composite letters reuse the modified base glyphs.
Original character coverage, shaping and spacing tables retained; altered outlines are unhinted.
Not yet production-qualified at small sizes or in a game engine.
